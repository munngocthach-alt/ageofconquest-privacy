Privacy Policy — Age of Conquest

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Age of Conquest (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The Game is developed by Thach Nguyen. By installing or playing the Game you agree to this policy.

Summary

The Game is a single-player, offline strategy game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any information you type. The Game shows advertisements, and the advertising provider collects certain device information to deliver and measure those ads. That is the only information that leaves your device.

Information stored on your device only

The Game saves your progress and preferences (levels completed, difficulty, music and sound-effect volume, and whether the intro has played) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and is removed if you uninstall the Game.

Information collected by our advertising provider

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) to display ads. To serve and measure ads, LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and other device identifiers
  • Device information (device model, operating-system version, language, network type)
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (not precise/GPS location)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks) and basic diagnostics

This information is used to show ads, limit repetition, measure ad performance, and detect fraud. It is collected and controlled by the advertising provider, not by us. See:

  • LevelPlay / ironSource Privacy Policy: developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/privacy-policy
  • On Android, reset or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages, precise location, microphone or camera data, financial or payment information, or any account or login data. The Game has no accounts and no social features.

Purchases

The Game offers one optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads, which turns off all advertising. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple’s App Store (iOS) — we never receive or store your payment details. Whether you have purchased it is remembered on your device and, through your store account, so you can restore it. Making a purchase is entirely optional; the full game is playable without it.

Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising provider, contact us and we will help address it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or limit ad tracking: Android — Settings → Google → Ads; iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete local data: uninstalling the Game removes all data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g., EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete personal data held by the advertising provider; those requests are handled through the provider linked above.

Data security and retention

Data transmitted by the advertising provider is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We do not hold any of this data ourselves. Retention is governed by the advertising provider’s policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected in a Game update.
