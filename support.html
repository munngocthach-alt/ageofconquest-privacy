Common questions

  • How do I remove ads? Tap Remove Ads on the Main Menu — a one-time purchase that permanently turns off all ads.
  • I bought Remove Ads on a new device / reinstalled. Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases; your purchase is tied to your store account.
  • My progress is gone. Progress is saved on the device; reinstalling or clearing the app's data resets it. There is no account or cloud save.
  • The game is free — how is it supported? By ads, unless you buy Remove Ads. There are no other purchases.
